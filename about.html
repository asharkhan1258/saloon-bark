About SB Service

For dogs and the people who love them.

Built around a simple idea: grooming can be gentle, stylish and enjoyable for pets and their people.

Our Story

From grooming service to full lifestyle experience.

SB Service began with a focus on better, more thoughtful grooming. Over time, that vision expanded into a destination that combines canine spa services with curated shopping and a welcoming community feel.

Today, the salon experience centers on individualized grooming, careful handling and finishing details that reflect each dog's personality.

Milestones

A business shaped by dogs, style and community.

2008 · The idea begins

A service-first approach focused on making grooming gentler and more personal.

2010 · Salon experience expands

A dedicated salon setting creates more room for professional care and connection with clients.

2012 · Boutique concept grows

Pet essentials, accessories and lifestyle products become part of the experience.

Today · A Fort Lauderdale favorite

Grooming, boutique shopping and pet-centered services continue under one stylish roof.
